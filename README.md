# Life-Expectancy-and-GDP

## 📌 Project Overview
This project aims to analyze data on GDP and life expectancy from the World Health Organization and the World Bank to try and identify the relationship between the GDP and life expectancy of six countries.

## Research Questions
*  Has life expectancy increased over time in the six nations?
*  Has GDP increased over time in the six nations?
*  Is there a correlation between GDP and life expectancy of a country?
*  What is the average life expectancy in these nations?

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
* **Language:** Python 3
* **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook
* **Libraries:** `matplotlib`, `pandas`, `seaborn`
* **Core Concepts:** Visualisations

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights
* There has been a significant increase in Life expectancy in Zimbabwe as compared to the other nations which have seen slight increases. 
* There has been a significant increase in the GDP of United States of America(USA) and China with the USA beating China by over 75%.
* Germany has seen very slow and insignificant growth.
* Chile, Mexico, and Zimbabwe have not had any growth within the evaluated time period. 
> ** Major Insight:**
> *  Zimbabwe has the lowest average Life Expectancy and GDP among the selected nations.
> *  There is weak correlation between GDP and Life Expectancy (**correlation coefficient = 0.34**)
